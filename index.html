A small idea with a big heart

A café for the
in-between moments.

Café Cahokia began as a simple idea: what if a neighbourhood café felt like a deep breath? Somewhere to meet a friend, read one more page, or enjoy a quiet moment before the day gets going.

We imagine a thoughtful little menu, ingredients chosen with the seasons, and a room that feels just as good on your own as it does with company. Nothing complicated. Just the everyday things, done with care.

A warm welcome, whenever you need one. ♡
